# Orbit

Orbit is a life dashboard: one screen that pulls together your day — calendar, reminders,
health, mail, weather, people, money, and whatever else you care about — instead of making
you open ten apps to find it. It runs on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, and Apple Watch.

Listed on the App Store as **Orbit Dashboard**.
